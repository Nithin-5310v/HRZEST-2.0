Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ legal_updated }}

We're reviewing this policy with legal advice. If anything changes, we'll update this page and its date.

HRzest (hrzest.com) is an HR platform that companies use to run attendance, leave, payroll, onboarding and performance for their employees. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and the choices and rights you have. It's written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • If you're an employee of a company that uses HRzest, your employer decides what data is collected about you and why. Under the DPDP Act your employer is the Data Fiduciary, and HRzest processes that data on its behalf as a Data Processor. Please contact your employer first about your data; we'll help them respond.
  • If you sign up a company, contact us or visit our website, HRzest is responsible for the data we collect from you.

2. What we collect

From companies that sign up

  • Company details: name, web address, GSTIN, logo, billing address and verification (KYC) documents.
  • The administrator's name, email address, mobile number and password. We store passwords only as a one-way hash and never see them.
  • Payment records. Payments are handled by Razorpay; we never receive or store your full card or bank account number.

About employees (entered or collected on the employer's behalf)

  • Identity and job details: name, employee ID, contact details, department, role, manager and joining date.
  • Attendance: check-in and check-out times, shifts, leave and holidays.
  • Face data, if the employer uses face check-in: a registered face photo and a face template derived from it, plus a photo taken at each check-in to confirm it's really you.
  • Location, if the employer uses geo-fenced check-in: your device's location at the moment you check in or out. We don't track your location at any other time.
  • Payroll: salary, deductions, bank details for salary payment, and payslips.
  • Documents you or your employer upload, onboarding tasks, and performance reviews.

Technical and security data

  • IP address, browser and device type, sign-in times and security events, used to keep accounts safe and to detect and block attacks.
  • Website traffic: we count how many times each public page is viewed per day. This uses no cookies and no tracking script, and doesn't store your IP address.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service your employer or company signed up for: attendance, leave, payroll, onboarding and performance.
  • To verify check-ins (face, QR code or location), so attendance records are accurate.
  • To create and secure accounts, send sign-in codes, password resets and service emails.
  • To bill companies and keep tax records.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent fraud and comply with the law.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Face and location data need consent

Face recognition and location-based check-in are used only where the employer has turned them on. Because this data is sensitive, the employer must tell its employees how it's used and get their consent before turning these features on. HRzest keeps a record of consent given and withdrawn. To withdraw your consent, tell your employer or email us; after that, face or location check-in won't be used for you, so ask your employer about another way to record attendance. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect check-ins made before it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos taken at check-in: 30 days.
  • Check-in verification records (for spotting fake or replayed check-ins): 180 days.
  • Registered face templates: while the person is employed there, and removed when their data is erased.
  • Attendance and payroll records: as long as employment, tax and labour laws require, after which they are deleted or no longer linked to a person.
  • Company account data: while the company uses HRzest, and afterwards as the law requires (for example, invoices).

7. How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sensitive personal fields are encrypted in the database, passwords are hashed, administrators use two-factor authentication, access is limited by role, and security-relevant actions are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

8. Cookies

We use only cookies needed for the site to work: a sign-in session cookie, a short-lived cookie that shows a loading screen after you sign in, and a short-lived cookie that protects "Sign up with Google/Microsoft" from forgery. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser's local storage also remembers a few display choices, such as a collapsed sidebar.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access your personal data, correct or update it, erase it, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. If you're an employee, ask your employer: HRzest gives employers tools to export or erase an employee's data. You can also email us at {{ seller.email }}. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

HRzest is for businesses and their employees. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date, and tell company administrators about important changes by email.

Security & vulnerability disclosure policy

We want HRZEST to be safe for the companies and employees who rely on it. If you think you've found a security vulnerability, please tell us. We'll work with you to fix it.

How to report

Email {{ contact_email }} with:

  • what you found and where (URL, endpoint or screen);
  • steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it;
  • your name or handle if you'd like to be credited.

Please don't include anyone's personal data in your report beyond what you need to show the problem.

What we ask of you

  • Test only against your own account or a test company you registered. Never access, change or delete another person's or company's data.
  • Don't run denial-of-service tests, spam, social engineering or physical attacks, and don't run high-volume automated scans against production.
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before telling anyone else about it.
  • Stop and tell us as soon as you reach data that isn't yours.

What you can expect from us

  • We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
  • We'll keep you updated while we investigate and fix it, and tell you when it's resolved.
  • We won't take legal action against research done in good faith that follows this policy.
  • With your permission, we'll credit you once the fix is live.

In scope

hrzest.com and its company portals, the HRZEST mobile app, and the HRZEST API.
